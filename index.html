USB Drivers & Connection Troubleshooting

Missing USB UART Drivers?

If your board's serial port does not show up in the selection dialog, install the driver corresponding to your board's USB-to-UART bridge chip:

CP2102 / CP2104 Driver CH340 / CH341 Driver FTDI VCP Driver

Board Fails to Enter Bootloader?

Some ESP32 boards (like NodeMCU-32S or ESP32-CAM) require manual bootloader entry:

  • Press and HOLD the BOOT (or IO0) button on your board.
  • Click Connect & Flash Device in the browser.
  • Release the BOOT button as soon as writing begins.
